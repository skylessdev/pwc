Noble Grapes in Dialogue with the Cape

Truth, like wine, is elucidated through dialogue.

Wine has always been more than simply the sum of its agricultural parts. It has been philosophy poured into a vessel: a record of landscape, season, memory and human intention.

The Platonist Wine Company exists to explore that tradition.

Rather than pursuing a single grape or a single style, we curate a symposium of the world’s noble varieties - Sangiovese, Cabernet Franc, Syrah, Grenache and others - asking what specific of their form they express and stories they carry from these ancient soils.

We invite you to join us in this intellectual endeavour.
